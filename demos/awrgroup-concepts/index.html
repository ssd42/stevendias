Website concepts

Four directions for awrgroupinc.com

Each concept takes the layout, typography and mood of one site you liked and rebuilds it around AWR's real content — the same nine services, the same landmark portfolio, the same photography. Only the design changes, so you're comparing design and nothing else.

Notes

Same content and the same photography in all four. Only the design changes, so you're comparing design and nothing else. All nine services with their real bullet lists, the full portfolio, the real About copy, and the real contact details (718.729.0412 / info@awrgroup.com).

The photography is AWR's own. Drone and site photos from 240 Centre Street, 200 Rector Street, 250 Cabrini Boulevard, 195 Bowery, 684 Broadway and 246 West End Avenue — re-cut for the web (1.1 GB of originals down to about 3 MB a page). Only three stock landmark photos remain, kept as the historic portfolio.

New projects are labelled by year, not by trade. We have addresses and dates for them but not confirmed scopes, so they read “2026” rather than listing services. The landmark entries keep the trade tags from AWR's current site.

Three claims are awaiting AWR's confirmation — “Family-run since 1947”, “Founded, and family-run ever since” and “Three generations, one standard.” Their current site says only “principals”. Public records confirm two Schindermans own the business and trace it back to Arrow Restoration in 1990, but nothing independent reaches 1947. Each line is flagged with a TO CONFIRM comment in the source.

Concept 04 is the one to look at first, and it is the only one with the per-project photo galleries.
