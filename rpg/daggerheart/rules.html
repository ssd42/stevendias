Daggerheart Comprehensive Reference

Quick reference for players - keep this open during your session!

The Core Mechanic: Duality Dice

Daggerheart uses two d12s called the Duality Dice - one Hope Die and one Fear Die.

d12 + d12 + Trait Modifier (+ Proficiency if experienced)

Hope Die Wins (or Tie)

Hope Die shows the higher number:

  • You gain 1 Hope
  • You describe the outcome
  • Action resolves in your favor

Fear Die Wins

Fear Die shows the higher number:

  • GM gains 1 Fear
  • GM describes complications
  • You may still succeed, but with a cost
Critical Success: Rolling doubles (both dice show the same number) is a Critical! You succeed dramatically with extra benefits.

Hope

Hope is your heroic currency - spend it to do incredible things!

Gaining Hope

  • Roll with Hope die winning (or tie)
  • Roleplay your character's bonds/values
  • GM awards for great moments

Spending Hope

  • 1 Hope: Add +1d6 to any roll
  • 1 Hope: Aid an ally (give them Advantage)
  • 2+ Hope: Activate certain abilities
  • Variable: Power domain cards

Fear (GM Resource)

Fear is the GM's resource to create tension.

GM Gains Fear When...

  • A player rolls with Fear die winning
  • Certain story events occur
  • Players take major risks

GM Spends Fear To...

  • Activate enemy abilities
  • Interrupt player actions
  • Introduce complications
  • Make GM moves
Action Tracker: Every roll adds a token. GM spends these to act with enemies. No "rounds" - just story flow.

Stress

Stress represents mental and emotional strain.

Gaining Stress

  • Witnessing traumatic events
  • Using certain powerful abilities
  • Some enemy attacks target Stress
  • Spell burns (failing with Fear)

Stress Slots

You have 6 Stress slots. When all 6 are marked, you gain a Scar and clear all Stress.

Clearing Stress

  • Taking a Rest
  • Connecting with party members
  • Certain abilities and items
Scars: Permanent marks of experience. May provide both benefits and complications.

Advantage & Disadvantage

Advantage

Roll 2d12 + 1d6. Add the d6 to your total (can reach up to 30!).

2d12 + 1d6 + Trait + Proficiency

Disadvantage

Roll 2d12 + 1d6. Subtract the d6 from your total.

Stacking: Multiple sources don't stack. If you have both advantage and disadvantage, they cancel out.

Experiences

If you have a relevant Experience, add your Proficiency bonus to the roll automatically.

Health & Damage

Taking Damage

  1. Optionally use Armor - Mark an Armor Slot to reduce damage by your Armor Score
  2. Compare remaining damage to your Thresholds (written on your sheet)
  3. Mark HP based on threshold hit
ThresholdDamage RangeHP Marked
MinorBelow your Minor threshold1 HP
MajorMeets/exceeds Minor, below Severe2 HP
SevereMeets/exceeds your Severe threshold3 HP
Armor Slots: Armor is a resource. You must mark an Armor Slot to apply your Armor Score. Once all slots are marked, you can't reduce damage until you rest.

At 0 HP (Death Moves)

  • Avoid Death: Roll vs DC, success = mark a Scar
  • Blaze of Glory: One final heroic action, then death
  • Accept Death: Give remaining Hope to allies

Origins: Ancestry & Community

Clank, Daemon, Drakona, Dwarf, Elf, Faerie, Faun, Firbolg, Fungril, Galapa, Giant, Goblin, Halfling, Human, Inferis, Katari, Orc, Ribbet, Simiah.

Communities

  • Highborne: Nobility and high society
  • Loreborne: Scholars and knowledge-seekers
  • Orderborne: Military or religious orders
  • Ridgeborne: Mountain and hill folk
  • Seaborne: Coastal and seafaring
  • Slyborne: Underworld and shadows
  • Wanderborne: Nomads and travelers
  • Wildborne: Forest and nature dwellers

Magic & Spellcasting

Spells are Domain Cards. You have a "Loadout" of active cards and a "Vault" for storage.

  • Spell Rolls: 2d12 + Trait vs Difficulty or Evasion
  • Passive: Always active while in Loadout
  • Active: Cost Hope or Stress to trigger
  • Continuous: Stay active until ended
Burn: If you fail a spell with Fear winning, GM may force you to mark Stress to keep it from fizzling.

Actions & Combat

Standard Actions

  • Attack: Roll vs Evasion
  • Cast: Use a domain card
  • Move: Change distance range
  • Sprint: Move extra far
  • Aid: Give ally Advantage (1 Hope)
  • Protect: Take damage for ally
  • Hide: Become hidden

Distances

  • Melee: Engaged, arm's reach
  • Close: Few steps away
  • Near: Same room/area
  • Far: Across a large room
  • Very Far: Edge of sight

Movement between ranges is usually free unless circumstances make it difficult.

Rests

Short Rest

A brief pause (~1 hour):

  • Recover some HP (roll recovery)
  • Clear 1-2 Stress
  • Clear Armor Slots
  • Swap domain cards

Long Rest

Full night's sleep in safety:

  • Recover all HP
  • Clear all Stress
  • Clear all Armor Slots
  • Refresh all abilities
  • Swap domain cards freely

Tips for New Players

Do...

  • Describe your actions cinematically
  • Spend Hope - it's meant to be used!
  • Lean into failure - Fear creates drama
  • Jump in - there's no turn order
  • Connect with party - it clears Stress

Remember...

  • Hope vs Fear matters as much as success/failure
  • Experiences add Proficiency when relevant
  • Armor Slots must be marked to reduce damage
  • Stress is separate from HP
  • Domain cards can be swapped during rests
